## Project artwork

The player preview and sixteen directional files in `assets/art/player/`, the thirty Patch 4.12 icons, the twenty Patch 4.15 material/defense/backpack files in `assets/art/shop/`, and the three Patch 4.15.1 runtime turret sprites in `assets/art/turrets/` are original assets generated with OpenAI image generation, chroma-key extracted, cropped, and reduced into transparent runtime PNGs. `assets/art/patch415/defense_material_atlas_source.png` preserves the Patch 4.15 production source. They do not intentionally reproduce a third-party game, character, logo, weapon, turret, or inventory system.

The six files in `assets/art/mobs/` are original mutant-spider assets generated for Patch 4.11 with OpenAI image generation and locally processed into transparent, palette-reduced runtime PNGs. They do not intentionally reproduce a third-party game, character, logo, or sprite sheet.

`assets/art/menu_background.png` and `assets/art/cave_material.png` are original AI-generated project artwork derived from the project's written brief and supplied concept images as mood, composition, and material references. They do not copy the supplied map or interface. `cave_material_runtime.png` is a nearest-neighbor runtime derivative of the generated cave material.

`assets/art/environment/phase2_environment_source.png` is original AI-generated project artwork created for the Phase 2 graphics pass from the game's visual brief. The three `*_material_runtime.png` files in the same folder are cropped, palette-reduced, nearest-neighbor runtime derivatives used for the hive, excavated tunnel, and bunker material families.

`assets/art/environment/phase3_environment_source.png` is an original AI-assisted Phase 3 revision of that material direction. The three `*_material_phase3.png` files are grid-aligned runtime derivatives used to preserve larger forms without directional compression.
