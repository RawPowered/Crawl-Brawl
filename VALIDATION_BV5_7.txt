Interpretation used for BV5.7
-----------------------------
- Mining mutation cost is already sub-millisecond; the visible mining hitch is surrounding frame workload.
- The hard 12/25 render/simulation envelopes are working.
- Terrain, lighting and ecology each retain 40-50 ms-class peaks and may stack in one display frame.
- The user observed the new-game growth/exploration window was too short before threat became overwhelming.
- Therefore BV5.7 targets opening threat pacing and heavy-phase isolation rather than another regional routing rewrite.
